Actions

Drag around the canvas to roll the wheel by hand. Scroll or pinch to zoom; Shift-drag or two fingers to pan.

Record video restarts the drawing and saves it as a video when it finishes. Save GIF makes a looping 6 second GIF of the whole drawing. Both are square.

Saved designs

Keeps the pattern, kept layers and picture on the server. Opens from a short link (Copy link on a saved design).

Background render

Renders run on the server one after another and wait here when done; closing the page is fine. PDF uses the page as set, with the Look tab's background; CMYK is a plain conversion without a colour profile.

Batch of names

Each name is drawn with the current Look settings and downloaded together as a zip. Also available as POST api/jobs.

Picture tools

The server downloads the address itself, so it can reach other homelab services. Split keeps the current picture's colours as separate layers, one outline each (the background colour is dropped). Cut out subject and Subject outline use a small neural network (U2-Net) to find the main subject, so the trace follows it and ignores the background. Face drawing finds the faces (InsightFace models). The sketch style keeps the photo's own edges (hair, lids, shading) inside the head outline and adds clean brows, eyes and mouth; the plain style draws only the jaw, brows, eyes, nose and mouth as lines. For big pictures, raise Detail on the Image tab up to 4096 and Circles up to 65000; tracing a large photo can take a minute.

Name

Press Play and a chain of rotating circles writes it out: a Fourier series of the letter strokes.

Image

The image's edges are traced as one line, then drawn by rotating circles. Works best on logos, silhouettes and clear outlines. The image stays in your browser.
