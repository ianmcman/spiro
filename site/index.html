Actions

Drag around the canvas to roll the wheel by hand. Scroll or pinch to zoom; Shift-drag or two fingers to pan.

Record video restarts the drawing and saves it as a video when it finishes. Save GIF makes a looping 6 second GIF of the whole drawing. Both are square.
